Data Deletion Instructions

Hướng dẫn yêu cầu xóa dữ liệu

Effective and last updated: October 9, 2026

You can request deletion of personal information controlled by Cốt Đờ Store in connection with its Messenger bot, support and order records. This page provides a manual request process; it is not an automatic deletion callback.

1. Send a request

Email accgame120700@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request - Cot Do Store, or send the administrator a message in your existing conversation with the Cốt Đờ Store Facebook Page.

Gửi email với tiêu đề trên hoặc nhắn trong cuộc trò chuyện với page: “Tôi yêu cầu xóa dữ liệu cá nhân mà Cốt Đờ Store lưu liên quan đến tài khoản và cuộc trò chuyện này.”

2. Provide only what is needed

Include your Facebook display name, a profile link or relevant order reference if available, and which information you want deleted. Prefer requesting from the original Messenger conversation so we can match your Page-scoped user identifier. If you email, we may ask you to verify the request through that conversation. Do not send passwords, login codes, full bank details or identity-document scans.

3. What we review and delete

  • The bot's temporary selection or confirmation session associated with your user identifier.
  • The persistent “fallback already sent” anti-spam flag associated with your user identifier.
  • Other identifiable support or order information held by the shop that is no longer required.
  • Relevant technical-log information, where deletion is supported by the provider. Logs or backups that cannot be selectively deleted remain subject to provider retention and applicable deletion requirements.

The administrator will review and verify your request, explain any information still needed, and confirm completion or applicable limits. Requests are handled within the period required by applicable law. Information necessary for a legal obligation, fraud investigation or unresolved transaction may need to be retained; any such limitation will be explained.

4. Limits and future messages

Deleting shop-controlled data does not automatically delete records independently held by Meta, your own Messenger history, hosting-provider security records or payment-provider records. Use those providers' own privacy controls for their records. If you message the bot again, a new session and new logs may be created; deleting the anti-spam flag may cause the fallback response to be sent once again.

5. Removing an app connection

If you personally authorized the app through Facebook Login, you can also review or remove that authorization in Facebook Settings under Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, where applicable. Removing an app connection and deleting shop-held records are separate actions.

6. Contact

Email: accgame120700@gmail.com. You may also contact the administrator through the Cốt Đờ Store Facebook Page conversation you used to reach the shop.
