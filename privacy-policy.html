Privacy Policy

Chính sách riêng tư

Effective and last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Cốt Đờ Store (“the shop”, “we”) handles information through our price-list website and Facebook Messenger bot. The bot provides product information, package selection, order confirmation and payment instructions; administrators handle support and order fulfillment.

1. Information we receive

  • Messenger Page-scoped user identifiers, message text, timestamps, button selections and attachment information supplied by Meta when you message the Page.
  • Package choices, product names, durations, quoted prices and confirmation or cancellation actions.
  • Contact details and payment or support information you voluntarily send to the administrator.
  • Technical request and error logs. Current webhook logs may include message contents, identifiers and attachment metadata.

The bot does not need your Facebook password, login code, payment-card security code or banking password. Do not send these or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, show prices, remember package selections, prevent repeated automated fallback messages, process and support orders, investigate errors and prevent abuse. Automated matching can be incorrect; ask an administrator to review an answer or quote.

3. Storage and retention

  • Temporary package-selection and confirmation sessions in Cloudflare KV expire after approximately 30 minutes unless refreshed or removed sooner.
  • A separate user-ID-based flag records whether the bot has sent its fallback response. This flag currently has no automatic expiration and remains until deleted by the administrator or upon a verified deletion request. It does not contain the message text.
  • Technical logs follow the hosting provider's configured retention. Messages in the Page inbox and administrator-held order or support records may remain longer as needed for support, disputes and legal obligations. The 30-minute session expiry is not a promise that all message or order data is deleted.

4. Providers and disclosure

Meta operates Facebook/Messenger; Cloudflare hosts the bot, sessions and technical logs; GitHub Pages hosts the public website. These providers may process information under their own policies and may process it in other countries. Shop administrators can access information needed for support. We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect against fraud or abuse.

The public website and these policy pages contain no customer chat records. These pages do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts; hosting providers may still keep access logs.

5. Your choices and requests

You can stop messaging the bot and request access, correction or deletion of information we control. Follow our data deletion instructions. We may verify that you control the relevant account before acting. Some records may need to be retained for a legal obligation or an unresolved transaction; we will explain applicable limitations. Meta-held information is subject to Meta's own controls and policies.

6. Security and updates

We restrict administrative access and use the security features of our providers, but cannot guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when the service changes; the updated date appears above.
